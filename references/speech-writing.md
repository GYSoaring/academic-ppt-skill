# Defense Speech Writing

Write for the ear, not for the page. The speaker should sound present, precise, and willing to state limits.

## Per-Slide Shape

Use this sequence without printing the labels in the final script:

1. What question this page answers.
2. What the audience is looking at.
3. What the figure, table, formula, or result supports.
4. What it does not support.
5. A short transition to the next page.

For a 20-minute defense, aim for 45 to 80 seconds per content slide. The cover and outline can be shorter.

## Natural Voice

- Use short spoken sentences and concrete verbs.
- Prefer `这里可以看到` and `需要说明的是` over ceremonial phrasing.
- Do not stack `首先、其次、再次、最后`.
- Do not read bullet text verbatim.
- Do not turn every pause into a rhetorical question.
- Avoid `卡脖子`, `从 0 到 1`, `国际领先`, `彻底解决`, and `全面胜出` unless the user supplies direct evidence and explicitly wants the phrase.

## Evidence Language

Use:

- `在这组实验条件下`
- `这组结果支持……`
- `它还不能证明……`
- `不同文献的系统参数和背景条件并不一致`
- `这里的提升来自等单脉冲能量口径`

Avoid:

- `显著优于所有方法`
- `完全消除`
- `不受背景影响`
- `实时且低功耗` without measurements
- `实验证明` when the material is only a simulation or concept diagram

## Figure Explanation

Every image needs four pieces:

1. What is shown: axes, objects, signal path, or experimental scene.
2. Why it is on this page.
3. Which claim it supports.
4. Which alternative explanation or limitation remains.

For a paper figure, do not invent curves or values. For a system diagram, call it a design or workflow, not a measured result. For a photograph, identify the setup and measurement condition before drawing conclusions.

## Claim, Evidence, Boundary

Keep these three fields distinct in `deck.json`:

- `claim`: the one-sentence takeaway.
- `evidence`: the exact figure, table, formula, or measured condition.
- `boundary`: what the evidence cannot establish.

The speech may connect them, but must not collapse them into one promotional sentence.

## Openings and Closings

Cover opening:

> 各位老师好，我是……，指导教师是……。今天汇报的题目是……。这次汇报主要回答一个问题：……

Closing:

> 以上就是本次汇报。结论可以概括为……，目前还没有覆盖……。谢谢各位老师，请批评指正。

Do not end with a new claim that has not been supported earlier.

## Timing

Estimate Chinese speech at roughly 210 to 240 characters per minute. Add 5 to 10 seconds for each unfamiliar figure. Keep 10 to 15 percent of the total time for pauses and transitions.
